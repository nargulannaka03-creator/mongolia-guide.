Mongolian festivals are,
mostly, a little crazy.

Tug-of-war on a lake frozen at −30°C. Rodeo on the back of a yak. Golden eagles called back to a rider's arm at full gallop. Here are the festivals where nomadic life turns into competition.

When can you go?

WinterSpringSummerAutumn

Dates change by year and region. Lunar New Year follows the lunar calendar, so it moves every year. Check the latest schedule before you go.
